# ShinTech
### QR-Based Smart Complaint Registration & Warranty Management System

ShinTech is a proposed QR-code-based system designed to simplify product complaint registration and warranty management. It aims to replace paper-based processes with a faster, more accessible digital experience.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Customers may lose physical warranty cards, struggle to find customer-care details, and experience delays when registering complaints. Companies may also receive incomplete complaints without sufficient product details, warranty proof, or evidence of the issue.

ShinTech aims to make after-sales support more efficient, paperless, and transparent.

## 💡 Proposed Solution

Each product is assigned a unique QR code. Customers scan the code using a smartphone to identify the product and access a complaint registration form.

The proposed workflow is:

1. Scan the product's QR code.
2. Identify the product and check its warranty information.
3. Submit a complaint with relevant details and images or videos.
4. Store complaint information in a cloud database.
5. Notify the assigned service engineer.
6. Track complaint status through the system.

## ✨ Planned Features

- Unique QR codes for product identification
- Digital warranty verification
- Complaint registration with image and video evidence
- Customer, administrator, and service engineer dashboards
- Service notifications and complaint status tracking
- Analytics and reports for complaint management
